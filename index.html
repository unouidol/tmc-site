PCG for beginners

Catch, collect, and play through Twitch chat.

Pokémon Community Game (PCG) is a free Twitch extension. Sign in once and your collection follows you across participating Twitch channels.

01

Open PCG

Join a channel with PCG enabled, open the extension, and sign in with your Twitch account.

02

Watch for a spawn

The PCG bot announces a wild Pokémon in chat. You have a limited time to decide whether to try catching it.

03

Choose one Poké Ball

Send !pokecatch or !pokecatch <ball> once through chat. Different balls suit different Pokémon, and every catch is chance-based.

04

Grow your collection

Successful catches join your collection and advance your Pokédex. Open the extension to review Pokémon, items, and other PCG activities.

How TMC helps

Your manual PCG companion on Android.

  • Keep Twitch chat and the PCG extension together in one app.
  • Update your ball inventory and missing Pokédex entries from the dedicated buttons while PCG is loaded.
  • Let Smart Catch Helper suggest suitable balls available in your inventory for the active spawn.
  • Use missing-Dex and Most Wanted alerts to notice the Pokémon that matter most to you.

TMC never catches automatically: every chat command or gameplay action must be triggered by you, one tap at a time.
